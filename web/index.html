What makes Coral different

Everyone can call a model. Almost no one does all of this at once — and no one does it as a native Mac app on your own plans.

⚡︎

No metering

Runs on the AI plans you already pay for. No per-token bill on top, no markup — your cost is your provider's cost.

🔒

Your keys, your Mac

No account, no server, no prompt routing. Your code and logins never leave this machine. Telemetry is off by default and local.

🧩

Mix providers per role

Plan with Claude, build with Codex, review with Gemini — one orchestrated run. No single-vendor CLI does cross-provider delegation.

🛡️

Reviewer ≠ author

An independent verifier — ideally a different provider family — gates autonomous loops and reviews an isolated worktree diff before you merge.

🧬

Per-line provenance

Every line is attributed to the model that wrote it, even across providers. Autonomous, but accountable by construction.

🗺️

Understands your code

The Map turns a repo into an interactive, agent-injected knowledge graph with estimated token savings — visual and portable, not a hosted vector index.

How it works

From a prompt to a running team in three steps — no setup ritual first.

STEP 1

Ask anything

Type your task like any chat, and optionally point it at a folder of code. Coral picks the right team — one agent or a specialized squad.

STEP 2

Watch the team work

The orchestrator plans, delegates to subagents and synthesizes — live, with tokens and cost per turn. No monthly surprise.

STEP 3

Ship it, verified

Run isolated in a git worktree, verify the diff with an independent reviewer, then merge — or deploy with a CLI you already have.
